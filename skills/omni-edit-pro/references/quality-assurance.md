# Quality assurance and delivery evidence

Use both automated inspection and human-style viewing/listening. Passing a build, parsing JSON, or following the skill's route does not establish editorial quality.

The dedicated **Production Video Reviewer** follows [Video reviewer](video-reviewer.md) alongside this checklist. Its acceptance standard is all applicable checks passed, verified wording and zero open observed defects, including tiny cosmetic errors. Severity affects repair priority only. This standard requires evidence; it is not a promise of perfect detection.

The independent reviewer verifies compliance with the resolved mode, output set, treatment and composition, then the actual viewing/listening result. Review each source and every requested output variant; a horizontal pass does not certify a vertical reflow or another scene layout. Use [Editing styles](editing-styles.md), [Local cutouts](local-cutout.md) and [Delivery](delivery.md) as acceptance contracts. Use [Evaluation](evaluation.md) for comparative release evidence; a job's QA pass alone is not proof that a skill revision improves video quality.

## Before rendering

Check the input-role inventory, reference-to-source map, explicit constraints/editing permissions and recorded output decisions. References and assets must not inflate source/variant counts. Confirm decision precedence and that All narrowing changed only the requested dimension. Adaptive/custom-ratio outputs must match the resolved plan; explicit presets retain their contracts.

Check the art-direction record for every actual source: portrait placement or explicit override, source/topic-specific graphic mechanism and motion vocabulary, transition reasons/anchors across the full duration, background/palette and subject/clothing/luminance contrast evidence. Do not approve a batch-wide copied card/fade/blue-background template merely because every source has its own folder. Same-source variants may share a coherent treatment; independent sources still need their own cue/design decisions within any requested brand/reference system.

Require named planned release checks for full-duration normal-speed viewing of every output, actual listening when audio exists, and Cutout playback on the actual animated, diagnostic light and diagnostic dark backgrounds when applicable. A compact plan still includes these entries. Later review records evidence/status separately; a vague contrast check or successful decode cannot silently count as those gates.

Also require the source-scoped approved terminology ledger, review of all displayed/delivered text, moving-face clearance, the vertical middle caption channel, padded badge containment across all text/animation states, and the reviewer's small-defect sweep. Render the longest/densest strings and difficult head/gesture movements in the representative test. An unverified word or known text collision cannot pass because the preview looks attractive overall.

Record the nine production requirements from the core: resolution-scaled presenter clearance, stationary semantic phrases, padded badge fit, measured heading centering, clean rendered/encoded text, verified entities/date/time, preserved original sound/sync, small source-anchored reveals with smooth handoffs, and panels matched to measured clear space. Plan the 2x graphics or evidenced equivalent path, actual quality-led encoding and source-stream handling. Require the reviewer's timecoded verification frames from the final file and clean-render comparison; a renderer setting is not visual evidence.

Require the per-unit slots in [Visual planning](visual-planning.md): exact small semantic content, first visibility tied to its verified anchor, settled/readable interval, exit/handoff, measured primary/compact representation and animated footprint. Prototype the narrowest clear region, longest required text, fastest semantic sequence, moving-gesture conflict and same-slot replacement. A single spacious still cannot validate this system. If the description does not fit, select its compact fallback or another permitted region before rendering; no unreadable shrinking or full future list with moving highlights.

Check intended source streams and timeline mode. In continuity mode, every original dialogue interval maps once, in order, at unchanged speed with original pauses. Confirm continuous base footage where applicable, including beneath supporting layers permitted by the composition. For non-dialogue material check preserved source sequence, content, action and intentional timing. Validate every authorized cut/reorder/speed or replacement against permission and the source-to-output map. Captions and original audio must follow the same map. Check sound ownership so source audio is neither duplicated nor accidentally muted; verify an intentionally silent path against the actual source.

Validate integer frame bounds, positive event durations, exact composition duration, source-time mappings, existing asset IDs, captions and layer ordering. Check cumulative duration and transition overlap if the user authorized an edited montage; transitions can change runtime. Ensure external assets have resolved rights and required credits. Remove placeholders before declaring a final output.

## Inspect preview and final

| Area | Evidence to collect | Failures to fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Narrative/content | Compare actual source and output at beginning, middle, end and every changed region; review the full output | Missing/repeated/reordered words or action, changed meaning, unauthorized cuts, removed intentional pauses, clipped tail |
| Sync/timing | Listen and watch lips where present; inspect captions, callouts and scene/action boundaries against source anchors | Constant offset, drift, incorrect word/action emphasis or premature/delayed reveal |
| Captions | Read at delivery size and playback speed; inspect every cue and line-break change | Wrong names/numbers/negations, missing glyphs, poor reading time, face overlap |
| Wording across text surfaces | Compare captions, titles, badges, lower thirds, end cards and delivered sidecars against the source-scoped verified terminology ledger and audio/authoritative evidence | Phonetic ASR guess promoted to fact, misspelling, wrong venue/landmark/acronym, inconsistent correction, unresolved term, changed spoken meaning |
| Face protection and middle captions | Inspect complete text/plate/highlight footprints through each cue, head movement and transition; verify the caption channel between upper animation and lower speaker in vertical presenter scenes | Any added text/graphic on a face, mouth or chin, including a one-frame contact; caption placed over lower footage by habit, unsafe fixed midpoint, ignored moving person |
| Badge/text containment | Measure and inspect actual loaded-font glyphs, strokes/diacritics and padding for every string, wrap and animated state at native/delivery size | Words or punctuation outside colored backing, clipped glyphs, fixed-width overflow, delayed backing resize, unreadable shrink, truncated approved wording |
| Subtitle stability | Compare complete-phrase box/word positions through all highlights and transitions; replay speech and check semantic boundaries and pauses | Sliding window, word-by-word recentering, highlight-induced bold/scale reflow, merged sentences, punctuation blindly splitting abbreviations, inaccurate active word or fake word timings |
| Heading centering | Compare actual rendered heading bounds/bearings to each banner/title/CTA content box with at most unavoidable one-pixel rounding difference | Eyeballed offsets, unequal unexplained margins, padding/icon ignored, shifting center when a word highlights; do not recenter intentional body/data layouts |
| Final typography/encode quality | Native-size decoded still/crop and normal-speed comparisons against clean pre-encode frames; actual render scale/filter and encode settings | Jagged diagonals/curves, blur, ringing/halos, chroma bleed, vanishing strokes, shimmer, banding, upscaled 1x raster labeled supersampled, fixed bitrate cap damaging larger output |
| Layout | View all states/boundaries and gestures at delivery size; in vertical presenter scenes check speaking footage/silhouette below and primary animation above in all four compositions unless explicitly overridden | Full-canvas/top speaker by habit, broken portrait ordering, cropped/too-small person, unreadable interface/cursor, clipped or obstructed labels, unsafe controls, changed composition |
| Motion/transitions | Watch the full duration and inspect the scene/cue coverage plan, evolving graphic states, text holds and transitions at normal speed | Long frozen card with only entry/exit, static default scene, arbitrary repeated fade/pulse/wiggle, missing source-specific progression, jitter/flashes/overshoot, unauthorized source cuts/retiming |
| Semantic-unit progression | Compare every unit's first semantic visibility and settled/readable state to verified speech/action anchors; inspect future rows before their cue | Paragraph/list dump, future descriptions exposed behind an active highlight, arbitrary timer stagger, separated negation/qualification, unreadably fast chunks or changed caption behavior |
| Smooth handoffs | Inspect adjacent entry/settle/exit and replacement frames, persistent-element transforms and normal-speed playback with actual audio where present | Hard pop, easing restart, one-frame flash/blank, truncated fade, old/new prose overprint, container/text mismatch, unsafe travel or premature next meaning |
| Available-space fit | Measure each active group's full animated footprint against contiguous clear regions, readable type floor and worst presenter/gesture clearance; inspect final delivery size | Large description forced into narrow side, tiny scaled text, oversized empty backing, needlessly small primary content, fixed side/template choice, lost breathing room or fit proven only at rest |
| Assets/color | Inspect source-grounded imagery and actual changing composites against the recorded topic, clothing/skin/hair, source lighting and brand/reference constraints | Universal blue/navy or palette cycling, no source-specific rationale, disappearing person/text, poor changing-background contrast, overstimulation, watermarks, stretch, wrong color transforms or misleading graphics |
| Audio/silence | Listen to actual candidate and source where audio exists, using useful monitoring equipment; measure selected targets; verify expected stream/silence when none is present | Echo, clipping, clicks, pumping, music masking, channel loss, unwanted added sound or a missing required track |
| Technical | FFprobe plus an end-to-end decode of the final file | Wrong codec/FPS/dimensions, corruption, unexpected stream start or duration |
| Framing/detail | Compare matching source/output times at delivery size, including gesture/action extremes and screen detail | Cover-fit crop, punch-in, stretched source, tiny presenter, enlarged soft face, unreadable source detail |
| Cutout | Inspect true alpha, spread-arm/gesture extremes, safe transparent gaps inside the bounding box, and full-canvas motion behind/around the subject on actual and contrasting backgrounds | Visible/backed rectangular cutout, box-shaped matte, reserved rectangular empty zone, unsafe essential text behind a person, missing hands/hair, flicker/holes/halos/spill or mask/RGB offset |
| Selection/delivery | Reconcile role-resolved sources, requested variant set, actual jobs and verified export paths | Reference counted as source, wrong composition, omitted variant, duplicate output or candidate labeled final |

Audit cue start, start+1, end-1 and end frames where valid, especially for nested sequences, caption replacement and scene-layout boundaries. Watch representative entries, holds, exits and transitions at normal speed, including the hardest motion. Sparse frame sampling complements playback; it cannot guarantee absence of every flash or dropped word. A transcript comparison helps locate omissions but cannot prove exact speech preservation because recognition itself can be wrong. For unchanged narration, compare source/output waveform timing at multiple points and investigate deviations beyond expected encode or gain differences. For silent/non-presenter scenes verify action/callout timing and visual continuity instead of requiring lip-sync evidence that cannot exist.

Follow [Video reviewer](video-reviewer.md) for the evidence manifest and post-render extraction: every scene transition, card hold, caption midpoint/boundary, difficult movement/highlight state and tail. Use the actual candidate fingerprint and lossless native-resolution crops. Verify minimum headroom across the full cue from the complete caption bottom, not from its anchor coordinate; for vertical default geometry apply [Layouts](layouts-effects.md). Check audio stream mapping and distinguish preserved content/timing from encoding identity; AAC bitrate is not proof of lossless preservation.

Review temporal coverage, not just attractive entry/exit frames. A moving source beneath an unchanged card does not by itself prove dynamic graphic treatment. Inspect the middle of each active graphic lifetime: the diagram, emphasis, relationship, mechanism or relevant local motion must continue to develop with the material. Stable readable labels can coexist with that development; record short reading holds and explicit stillness overrides, with interval/reason. Reject prolonged frozen explanations and decorative perpetual motion that has no semantic purpose. Source transitions remain subject to edit permissions; use graphic-state transitions when the source must stay continuous.

For color, review both light/dark and actual scene backgrounds over the changing effect, including difficult clothing/hair edges and caption states. Record actual chosen background/accent/text roles and the source/contrast reason. A named topic alone is not enough to assert readable contrast, and different hex colors alone are not proof of individually designed videos. Explicit blue branding may pass after contrast checks; a routine blue fallback without evidence may not.

## Treatment comparison and deviations

Before extending a treatment, review a representative rendered test against actual source evidence and, in Reference mode, corresponding sample passages at intended viewing size. Normally 15–30 seconds is useful; include the dense passage and hardest relevant effect even when that requires a different length or several short intervals. Listen where sound exists. Record the test's file/version, source/reference intervals, coverage and verdict. A still or contact sheet cannot establish motion or audible resemblance.

For Reference, keep an attribute comparison record for each relevant dimension: typography/captions, layout/framing, graphic density/color, motion/transitions, pacing, and sound. Include reference timecode/evidence/confidence, source/output interval, intended adaptation, actual observation, and verdict (matched, adapted, failed or unassessed). Distinguish measured from inferred findings. A perceived similarity is a reviewer judgment, not proof of exact original settings. Missing playback/audio evidence makes that dimension unassessed, never matched.

Record deliberate deviations and their reason: explicit user shape, preset composition, no-crop requirement, preserved dialogue/timing, source detail, cleared-asset limits or tool capability. For example, a rapid-cut sample plus keep-every-word can justify typography/motion adaptation while its cut rhythm remains deliberately unmatched. Evaluate observable requested attributes within those constraints; do not conceal material differences behind a technical pass or an overall match claim. An unavailable required reference blocks verified Reference completion; obtain the missing evidence or use an explicit user-authorized change/fallback before changing modes, and report it. Existing fallback authorization does not need to be requested again.

For Auto, judge source-grounded clarity, relevance, pacing and restraint against the recorded reason and actual content. For Manual/presets, verify supplied choices and their viewing result. Review full candidates and every resolved variant after the short test; one passage or one orientation cannot certify the remainder.

An example full decode check with installed FFmpeg is:

```text
ffmpeg -v error -i "out/final.mp4" -map 0:v:0 -map 0:a? -f null -
```

Adapt stream selection for the actual streams, including a genuinely silent video. Successful decoding does not certify sync, rights, reference resemblance or design. Listen through the final tail where audio exists and inspect actual final frames; container duration alone can hide clipped speech or an unintended frozen ending.

## Release decision

Fix every observed defect, including small cosmetic failures, and rerender the affected preview and candidate final as needed. Recheck adjacent intervals and dependent layouts. Every replacement full candidate requires full-duration normal-speed viewing and applicable listening again; reuse unchanged source/rights research rather than repeating unrelated work. The reviewer closes defects only against the actual repaired export. Apply the bounded recovery rules in [Environment](environment.md): after two targeted failures on the same cause, diagnose a different free/local path or surface the specific unresolved need. Continue independent variants without lowering acceptance criteria.

If required visual, motion, listening or reference-comparison review is unavailable, mark the candidate needs-review and keep it in `Working Files` with exact clips/intervals for assessment. A verified silent source does not require nonexistent lip-sync/audio content; silence and stream status still need inspection. Do not promote an uninspected export or claim a verified match. User review can supply missing evidence; lack of review never counts as a pass.

Record each required check as passed, failed or not run, with file/version and concrete evidence. Mark inapplicable checks explicitly with a source-grounded reason. Keep technical pass/fail separate from treatment judgments and comparison coverage. Report unresolved problems and the smallest next action. A preview, partial variant set or unassessed required attribute remains incomplete and must be labeled accurately.

Attach the reviewer task identity, exact candidate fingerprint, coverage and zero-open-defect verdict under [Video reviewer](video-reviewer.md). There is no pass with minor issues. A source-embedded defect that cannot be fixed within authorized edits still blocks affected completion until resolved; it is not silently exempted or masked.

Source: [FFmpeg documentation](https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg.html).
